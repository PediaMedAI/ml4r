Megan Coffee is an infectious disease physician and Clinical Assistant Professor at NYU Grossman School of Medicine, where she treats patients at Bellevue Hospital, and an Assistant Professor of Population and Family Health at the Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health. She uses mathematical modeling and machine learning to forecast, detect, and respond to disease in low-resource settings, including AI tools for tuberculosis radiology and clinical severity prediction. She directed a tuberculosis ward in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, for four years and coordinated Ebola treatment units in Sierra Leone for the International Rescue Committee. She holds an MD from Harvard and a PhD from the University of Oxford.
