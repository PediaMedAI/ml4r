Dennis Wall is a Professor of Pediatrics (Clinical Informatics) and of Biomedical Data Science, and Professor (by courtesy) of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences, at Stanford University. His Pediatric Innovation lab develops ML and mobile methods for fast, quantitative detection and treatment of childhood-onset neurodevelopmental conditions such as autism and developmental delay. His work includes wearable and game-based digital therapies, such as the Superpower Glass trials and the GuessWhat mobile game. He is also a co-founder of Cognoa, a company that develops solutions for pediatric developmental health including an FDA authorized autism diagnostic for children that uses AI. He received his PhD from UC Berkeley and was on the faculty of Harvard Medical School before joining Stanford.
