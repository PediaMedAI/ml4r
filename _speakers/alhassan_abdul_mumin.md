Alhassan Abdul-Mumin is a Senior Lecturer in the School of Medicine and Health Sciences at the University for Development Studies and a Consultant Pediatrician at Tamale Teaching Hospital in Tamale, Ghana. He heads the Department of Pediatrics and Child Health at both institutions. His research covers general child health, child nutrition, congenital anomalies, and neonatology, with the goal of improving care and outcomes for children in low-resource settings.
