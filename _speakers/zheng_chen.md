Zheng Chen is a Specially Appointed Associate Professor at SANKEN, Osaka University, Japan, where he leads a research group at the intersection of machine learning, biomedical engineering, and bioinformatics. His group formulates biomedical problems as machine learning tasks, including EEG-based brain dynamics modeling and disease prediction, multi-omics analysis and knowledge graphs, and agentic AI for automated biomedical data analysis. His recent work has appeared at ICLR, NeurIPS (Spotlight), AAAI, Nature Biomedical Engineering, and Scientific Data.
