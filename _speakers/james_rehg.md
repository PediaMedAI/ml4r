James M. Rehg is a Founder Professor of Computer Science and of Industrial and Enterprise Systems Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Previously, he was a Professor at Georgia Tech, where he co-directed the Center for Health Analytics and Informatics. His research in computational behavioral science uses computer vision and wearable sensing to measure children's social and communicative behavior, which is directly relevant to behavioral phenotyping of rare neurodevelopmental conditions. He served as General Chair of CVPR 2009 and Program Chair of CVPR 2017. He and his students have received many paper awards, including best student paper awards at ICML 2005, BMVC 2010, MobiHealth 2014, and Face and Gesture 2015, a Distinguished Paper Award from ACM IMWUT, and a Method of the Year award from Nature Methods.
