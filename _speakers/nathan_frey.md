Nathan C. Frey leads drug discovery and development research at Anthropic in New York, where his team builds AI systems that help scientists create new medicines. He previously co-founded Coefficient Bio, was a group leader at Genentech working on lab-in-the-loop therapeutic antibody design and foundation models for drug discovery, and was a postdoctoral fellow at MIT. His work on protein discovery with discrete walk-jump sampling received an Outstanding Paper Award at ICLR 2024. He received his PhD in Materials Science and Engineering from the University of Pennsylvania as an NDSEG Fellow.
