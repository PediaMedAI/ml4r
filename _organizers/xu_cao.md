Xu Cao is the director and tech lead of PediaMed AI Lab. His long-term research interests are AI for pediatrics, autism, and neurodevelopmental disorders. He is the 2026 Google PhD Fellow in Machine Perception and CIFAR Next Generation Trainee PhD Fellow in Child & Brain Development. As a former pediatric rare disease patient, he is strongly motivated to use AI to address rare diseases. He was the founder and general chair of the AI4CHL workshop at ICLR 2025 and the CV4CHL workshop at CVPR 2026, and served as the research roundtable subchair at ML4H 2024 and area chair of NeurIPS 2026.
