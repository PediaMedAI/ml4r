Marie Amale Huynh is a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Data Science at Stanford University, advised by Prof. Serena Yeung-Levy, and is also pursuing an M.S. in Computational and Mathematical Engineering. Her research focuses on developing reliable AI methods for healthcare and child development, with work spanning digital phenotyping of autism, nonverbal social reasoning in early childhood, and robust vision-language models. Her work has appeared in NeurIPS and Algorithms.
