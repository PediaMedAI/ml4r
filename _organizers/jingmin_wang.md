Jingmin Wang is a Professor of Pediatrics at Peking University First Hospital specializing in pediatric neurology and rare genetic neurological disorders. In 2005, she and her team began China's first research on megalencephalic leukoencephalopathy with subcortical cysts (MLC), a rare leukodystrophy. They reported the first Chinese cases with MLC1 (2006) and GlialCAM (2012) mutations, built one of the largest national patient cohorts, and developed cellular and mouse models, prenatal molecular diagnosis, and genetic counseling for affected families.
