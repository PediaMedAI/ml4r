Houze Yang is a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Inki Kim. His research focuses on social behavior modeling, using computer vision and multimodal learning to understand gaze, social attention, and human interaction, as well as interactive AI and extended reality. His work has appeared in CVPR, EMNLP, and HCI International.
