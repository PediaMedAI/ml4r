Meihuan Huang is a physiotherapist at Shenzhen Children's Hospital with more than 10 years of experience in pediatric rehabilitation. Her research focuses on validating clinical outcome measures and on pediatric pain in neuromuscular diseases, a major group of rare diseases. She leads funded studies on chronic pain and on the validation of the Chinese version of the Motor Function Measure (MFM-32-CN), and she has received national awards for her work. She holds a PhD from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
