Laura Bravo-Sánchez is a Machine Learning Engineer at Apple. She received her PhD from Stanford University advised by Prof. Serena Yeung-Levy. Her PhD research focused on computer vision to study human behavior and social interaction, with applications to healthcare and child development. Her work has appeared in Science Advances, CVPR, ECCV, NeurIPS, 3DV, and MICCAI.
