Daphné Chopard is a senior research scientist at University Children's Hospital Zürich, Switzerland, where she leads AI research within the Pediatric and Neonatal Intensive Care Research Group. Her work lies at the intersection of machine learning and pediatric medicine, with a focus on rare-disease identification using clinical and multi-omics data. She is also actively involved in fostering interdisciplinary research at the intersection of AI and child health, having contributed to the organization of the AI in Pediatric Health track at AMLD 2025 and the CVPR 2026 Workshop on Computer Vision for Children.
