Yoko Tabe is a Professor in the Department of Next Generation Hematology Laboratory Medicine and the Department of Clinical Laboratory Medicine at Juntendo University Graduate School of Medicine in Tokyo, and has been an Adjunct Professor in the Department of Leukemia at MD Anderson Cancer Center since 2020. She trained at MD Anderson and the U.S. National Cancer Institute. Her research covers AI-based blood cell analysis, deep learning diagnostic support for rare hematologic neoplasms, and the metabolism of leukemia cells in their microenvironment. She has published more than 100 peer-reviewed articles.
